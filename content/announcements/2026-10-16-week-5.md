**Monday, October 19 · [Week 5: Nested Quantifiers and Specifications](/weeks/week-05).** Last week every statement had one quantifier. This week we put one inside another and discover that their order changes everything: “everyone follows someone” is an ordinary fact about almost any social network, while “someone is followed by everyone” claims there is a celebrity. The notes include an interactive grid where you can see the difference as rows versus columns, and they show how engineers use the same ideas to write precise specifications for programs. Reading: Rosen 1.5.
