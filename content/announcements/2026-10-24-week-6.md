**Monday, October 26 · [Week 6: Rules of Inference](/weeks/week-06).** So far we have asked whether a statement is true. This week we ask whether it **follows** from other statements, which is what every proof, every diagnosis and every good debugging session does. We meet the eight rules from which valid arguments are built, two fallacies that look convincing but are not, the rules for “for all” and “there exists”, and finally the reason the Socrates argument from week 4 is valid. The notes include an **argument checker**: type the premises and a conclusion, and it shows you the critical rows of the truth table, or the row that breaks the argument. Reading: Rosen 1.6.

**Lab 5 · [A Tiny Inference Engine](/labs/lab-05)** opens right after the lecture. You will build a small reasoning program: a validity checker, a forward-chaining engine that applies modus ponens until nothing new appears, explanations of how it reached each conclusion, backward chaining, and a course advisor written as rules. Follow the steps in order; each one has an example and the expected result. Every push is graded out of 100, and you can also [submit from the browser](/labs/lab-05#other-ways-to-work-and-submit). **Due: Friday, October 30, 23:59.**
