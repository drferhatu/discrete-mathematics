**Monday, October 12 · [Week 4: Predicates and Quantifiers](/weeks/week-04).** Propositional logic cannot say “**every** student” or “**some** server”. This week we add the two symbols that can, ∀ and ∃, and learn to translate English into logic without the two classic mistakes. The notes contain an interactive **Quantifier Lab**: click the data and watch a “for all” statement become true or false. Reading: Rosen 1.4. Bring paper and a pen.
